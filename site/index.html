FAQ

What people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and no card. It covers what a one-person business needs: jobs, quotes, photos, customers and the consumer-law documents. Pro adds the team and documentation features.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the link in Safari or Chrome and choose “Add to Home Screen”. It then opens full-screen, works offline and gets updates automatically. App Store and Google Play versions follow.

Where is my data?

On your device, by default. Nothing is uploaded until you turn on sync (Pro). Export everything as PDF or CSV at any time – your data is yours.

Which countries and languages?

Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish and German from day one, with currency of your choice. Legal documents are Norway-first; UK, Swedish, Danish and German versions are on the roadmap.

Does it replace my accounting software?

No – and it shouldn't. Ordra produces the invoice basis with everything the bookkeeper needs; your accounting system issues the invoice. Direct integrations are coming.

What about the AI features?

They're being built. Every AI action lands in an approval queue – a quote draft, a follow-up message, an invoice basis – and nothing leaves the app until you tap approve.
