Why Ordra exists

Quotes in text threads. Photos in the camera roll. Agreements in your head.

Most office tools are built for someone sitting at a desk. The person on the job needs something else: one folder per job, in your pocket, that shows what was agreed when the customer has forgotten. That is what Ordra is. The basic version is free.

FAQ

What people ask

Is it really free?

Yes. The Free plan has no time limit and no card. It covers what a one-person business needs: jobs, quotes, photos, customers and the consumer-law documents.

Do I need to install anything?

No. Open the link in Safari or Chrome and choose “Add to Home Screen”. It then opens full-screen like any app.

Where is my data?

On your device. In the free version nothing is uploaded, so take a backup now and then.

Which countries and languages?

The app is available in Norwegian, English, Swedish, Danish and German. You change language in the app under More. Legal documents follow Norwegian rules. This website is available in Norwegian and English.

Does it replace my accounting software?

No – and it shouldn't. Ordra produces the invoice basis with everything the bookkeeper needs; your accounting system issues the invoice.

What about the AI features?

The AI features and the assistant Jarvis are being developed and tested before release. The version available now has no AI.
